Technical Risk Management & Mitigation Matrix: Addressing Evaluator Concerns
IDENTIFIED RISK POTENTIAL IMPACT PLATFORM MITIGATION STRATEGY VERIFICATION STATUS
1. Limited Qubit Count Cannot directly encode thousands of genes or pixels. Supervised PCA & Bottleneck Projections (8–16 components) with layer-wise feature re-uploading. Verified (8 & 10 Qubit circuits)
2. Quantum Noise & Decoherence Distorts quantum expectations and gradient degradation. Parameter-shift analytic differentiation avoids finite-difference noise; shallow circuits (L ≤ 5). Verified on PennyLane autograd
3. High-Dimensional Features Curse of dimensionality, memory bloat, overfitting. Classical deep transfer learning backbones (DenseNet-121) extract compact latent vectors before PCA. Verified on HAM10000 cohort
4. Circuit Execution Latency High cloud hardware cost and latency queues. Offline statevector simulator runs locally (<1.2 GB RAM, <200 ms latency) without cloud queue bottlenecks. Verified local execution
5. Absence of Quantum Advantage Quantum circuit underperforms classical ML. Honest Quantum Advantage Score (QAS) reporting; classical baselines maintained as production fallbacks. Verified in registry.json
