## Executive Positioning & Core Tenets

1. **Practical NISQ-Era Realism:** Replaces theoretical quantum claims with a hybrid architecture executable today on quantum simulators (8–12 qubits) with near-term hardware queue compatibility.
2. **Strict Baseline Benchmarking:** No claim of quantum superiority is made without direct empirical comparison against tuned classical baselines across Accuracy, Sensitivity, Specificity, F1-score, and Latency.
3. **Clinical Research & Decision Support:** Positioned strictly as an investigational and decision-support technology for researchers and clinicians, not an autonomous diagnostic replacement.
4. **Data Privacy & Governance:** Engineered for on-premise, air-gapped deployment with 18-point HIPAA de-identification and India DPDP Act 2023 consent compliance.
